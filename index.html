Fernando Lions is an artist and tattooer born in Mexico, currently working out of his private studio Street Dreams in Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn. Known for his distinctive yet classic take on New York style tattooing, graffiti, and design, Lions draws inspiration from world travel, counterculture, fine art, and the characters that shape the city.

Follow his work at @lionsnyc and @streetdreamsstudio.
