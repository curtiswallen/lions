FERNANDO LIONS

Fernando Lions is an artist and tattooer based in Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn, where he works out of his private studio Street Dreams. Known for his distinctive yet classic take on New York style tattooing, graffiti, and design, Lions draws inspiration from world travel, counterculture, fine art, and the characters that shape his city.

Follow his work at @lionsnyc and @streetdreamsstudio.

For inquires and commissions please contact studio@streetdreams.nyc.
